LEVEL 2 · FLOAT & PILOT

Adjust buoyancy. Predict movement.

Target: A standard SeaPerch should rise slowly when released underwater with its motors off. This is slightly positive buoyancy.

Observe → adjust → retest

ObservationAdjustment to test
Sinks with motors offAdd a little flotation or reduce removable ballast.
Rises too quickly; hard to diveReduce flotation slightly or add a little secured ballast.
Rises slowlyNear the target. Check balance and control.
Tilts to one sideAdjust float or weight placement to improve trim.

Ballast means added weight. Trim means balance. A level ROV can still have too much or too little buoyancy.

Make one small change at a time. Keep weights secure and use teacher-approved materials. Retest in your operating water.
